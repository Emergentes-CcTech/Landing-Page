About IoBuild

IoT technology for more transparent condominiums

Our mission

That every owner understands and trusts what they pay, and that every management team has real data to look after their building's water and energy.

To do that, we combine non-invasive IoT sensors in common areas, exact proration under Peruvian Law 27157 and fees explained in plain language, so the maintenance fee stops being a number argued over at every meeting.

Our values

Transparency

Every sol of the fee has an explanation the owner can review.

Precision

We measure instead of assuming, and the proration adds up to the cent.

Simplicity

Tools management can use without training and owners understand at first glance.

Sustainability

A leak caught in time is water not lost and money not paid.
