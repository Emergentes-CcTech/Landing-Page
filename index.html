How it works

From activation to the first explained fee, in four steps

  1. 1

    Register and activate your condominium

    The plan is assigned automatically by the number of apartments. At activation you pay the first and last month of the contract.

  2. 2

    We install the IoT kit

    A 4G gateway, flow meters and current clamps on the cistern, pumps, elevators and common panel. No construction work and no extra cost.

  3. 3

    Load the unit registry and register the owners

    Import the units from Excel (CSV). Shares come from the bylaws or are calculated by area. Each owner creates an account with the email you registered.

  4. 4

    Every month, proration in one click

    You enter the water and power bills, IoBuild calculates each unit's fee to the cent, and owners see it explained and pay online.

Why IoBuild

Fewer arguments about the fee and less wasted water

Leaks caught at night

If the flow doesn't drop between 1 and 4 a.m., you get an alert before the bill arrives.

A statistical detector also learns your building's night pattern and warns when a night falls outside it.

Exact proration by ownership share

Each unit pays by its share (Peruvian Law 27157), adding up to exactly 100% with no rounding errors.

Fees that explain themselves

Owners see what the building spent, their share, what it's made of and what changed from last month.

Reconciled bills

Compare what was measured with what Sedapal and Luz del Sur billed, and keep the scanned bill with each settlement.

Non-invasive installation

Sensors installed without cutting water or power. Installation is on us.

Online payments

Owners pay their fee by card, and management instantly sees who is up to date.
