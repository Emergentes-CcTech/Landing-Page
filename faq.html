What property managers and owners usually ask

It's a platform to manage condominiums. It measures the water and energy of common areas with IoT sensors, detects leaks and calculates each unit's maintenance fee by its ownership share (Peruvian Law 27157). Each owner sees their fee explained and can pay it online.

For property managers and owners' boards of residential buildings of any size: there are plans for up to 40, 41 to 80 and more than 80 apartments. Owners use the platform to see and pay their fee.

No. IoBuild measures the common areas: the cistern, pumps, elevators and common services panel. That common expense is split among the units by their ownership share, as the law requires.

From the building's bylaws, registered with SUNARP. If you don't have them at hand, IoBuild calculates it from each unit's area and makes the total add up to exactly 100%.

A 4G gateway with local reading backup, flow meters for the cistern and current clamps for the electrical panels. The number depends on the plan: 3 devices in Esencial, 5 in Urbano and 7 in Gran Torre. They are provided on loan.

No. The sensors are non-invasive: they go on the existing pipes and cables. Our team does the installation and it's included in the plan.
